Privacy Policy

@appName@ (@domain@) is a private, self-hosted Google OAuth client used exclusively by its owner to synchronize Gmail messages and labels locally via Lieer and Notmuch.

Data Accessed

The application requests the Gmail API scopes required by Lieer (gmail.readonly, gmail.labels, and gmail.modify) solely to download messages and synchronize label state between Gmail and the owner's local mail store.

Data Storage & Sharing

  • Email data and OAuth tokens are stored exclusively on the owner's personal devices and are never transmitted to this web server.
  • No Google user data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising, analytics, or AI/ML model training.

Revocation & Deletion

Access can be revoked at any time from your Google Account permissions, and local copies can be deleted directly from the local mail directory.

Contact

Questions regarding this policy can be directed to @contactEmail@.
