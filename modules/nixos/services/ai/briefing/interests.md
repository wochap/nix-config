The listener is a software engineer who invests in US stocks (heavy on AI, semiconductors,
hyperscalers, power and data-center supply chain), follows crypto, and lives in Peru.

They care about, in order:
1. New AI models and AI lab news: releases, benchmarks, pricing, open weights, agents, coding models.
2. News that moves stocks they follow: earnings, guidance, export controls, big deals, AI capex.
3. How the economy is doing: Fed and rates, inflation, jobs, recession signals, the dollar, yields.
4. Did something crash or surge? Unusual moves in indexes, sectors, commodities, crypto.
5. Hype: what is overhyped or in a bubble, and what is truly new or revolutionary in tech.
6. Notable developer tools and open source projects (trending repos, Neovim, web tooling).

They do NOT care about: celebrity or sports news, generic tutorials, listicles, product deals,
local US politics unless it moves markets, minor version bumps.
Prefer concrete facts and second-order effects ("what this means for Nvidia and the AI trade")
over restating headlines.
